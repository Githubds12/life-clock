About Life Clock

Life Clock is a visual reflection of mortality and time. The 3D hourglass represents your lifespan. The sand in the top bulb is the time you have left; the sand in the bottom is the time you have lived.

By confronting the reality that time is limited, we are inspired to live more purposefully, love more deeply, and cherish every passing moment.

"Time is free, but it's priceless. You can't own it, but you can use it."
— Harvey Mackay
